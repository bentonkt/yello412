Last updated November 10, 2026

{{businessName}} is an answering service. When a business that uses us misses a call, our line answers in that business’s name, takes down the caller’s details and texts them to the business. This page explains what we collect while doing that and what we do with it. {{businessName}} is run by {{ownerName}} in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

If you call a business that uses {{businessName}}

The call is recorded and transcribed, and the line tells you so when it answers. We collect what you tell us and what your phone sends:

  • your name, callback number, address or area, what you need and when you want it
  • the number you called from and the time of the call
  • the recording and transcript of the call

We use this only to pass your message to the business you called and to keep the service working. The business gets a text with your details and can see a summary of the call. We don’t sell your information or use it to market to you.

If your business uses {{businessName}}

We keep your business name, the people who receive texts and their cell numbers, your hours and service area, and a record of each call we answer for you. We use them to run your line, send your texts and bill you.

Text messages

Owners and staff who sign up get texts about calls we answer: one text per call, plus a weekly summary and, for emergencies, a phone call. How many texts you get depends on how many calls come in. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts and HELP for help.

We don’t share mobile numbers or text-message opt-in information with anyone for their marketing.

How long we keep it

Call recordings and full transcripts are deleted after 30 days. Call summaries are kept while the business uses {{businessName}} so it can look back at past calls, and are deleted when the business asks or leaves.

Who helps us run the service

We use a few providers to answer calls, send texts and host our systems: Retell AI (voice), Twilio (texts and calls) and Cloudflare (hosting and data storage). They handle data for us under their own privacy and security terms, and only to provide their service.

This website

This site has no forms and sets no cookies. Our host may log basic request data, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure.
